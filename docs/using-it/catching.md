# Catching mobs

Every kind of mob has its own bucket. Catch the mob, carry it in your
inventory, and let it out wherever you want it.

## Step 1 - Craft the bucket

Put these anywhere in a crafting table, in any order:

* **1 empty bucket**
* **3 slime balls**
* **3 of the mob's item**, for example rotten flesh for a zombie, bones for a
  wolf, emeralds for a villager

Every recipe is in your recipe book. Or type the command with the mob's name:

```
/mobbuckets recipe villager
```

Whole stacks are fine, and shift-click crafts as many as you have materials
for. Empty mob buckets of the same kind stack up to 16.

## Step 2 - Catch the mob

**Right-click the mob** with its bucket. It works **half the time**. When it
misses, the bucket breaks and the mob stays where it is, so bring a spare.

## Step 3 - Let it out

**Right-click a block** with the full bucket. The mob comes out on the side
you clicked, like with a spawn egg, facing you. It needs room to stand: a horse
won't come out under a low ceiling. You are left with a plain empty bucket,
ready for the next recipe. A mob
you let out never wanders off by despawning, even a zombie.

## Better odds

Strengthen an empty mob bucket in a brewing stand and it catches **three tries
in four** instead of half:

1. Put the mob bucket where the potion bottles go.
2. Put **redstone dust** on top.
3. Fuel the stand with blaze powder, as usual.

A strengthened bucket glows. Each bucket can only be strengthened once.

## Dangerous mobs

Blazes, breezes, wither skeletons, shulkers, hoglins, zoglins, piglin brutes,
ravagers and evokers need a **reinforced bucket**: 3 slime blocks and 2 chains
instead of the slime balls, plus 3 of the mob's item as usual.

## What the mob keeps

| Comes along | Stays behind |
| --- | --- |
| Its name and whether it is a baby | Items in a donkey's, mule's or llama's chest: they drop on the ground when it is caught |
| Its health, colours and patterns | |
| Who tamed it | |
| A horse's speed and jump | |
| A villager's job, level and trades | |

A full bucket shows what is inside: the mob's name, a baby, its owner or a
villager's job.

## When it won't work

| Situation | Why |
| --- | --- |
| **Somebody else's pet** | Only the owner can catch a tamed pet. |
| **In somebody else's claim** | You need their container trust to catch an animal, pet or villager there, and build trust to let a mob out. Monsters can be caught anywhere. |
| **On a lead, carrying a rider or riding something** | Free it first. |
| **A villager you are trading with** | Close the trading window first. |
| **A mob made by another plugin** | Custom bosses and shop keepers can't be caught. |
| **The wrong bucket** | A cow bucket only catches cows. The message tells you which mob the bucket is for. |

Some mobs can never be caught: the Wither, the Ender Dragon, the Warden,
guardians, ghasts, the creaking, vexes, silverfish and endermites. Fish,
axolotls and tadpoles already have the normal water bucket.
